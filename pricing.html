How Your Security Deposit Works

Total transparency from booking authorization to safe return release.

1. Temporary Pre-Auth

When you complete your rental, a temporary pre-authorization hold is placed on your card ($40 for Kids, $60 for Themed Parties, $90 for Cultural, $100 for Historical Armor). Funds do not leave your account permanently.

2. Scan In Receipt

Drop the garment in the pre-addressed return box at any courier point. The moment the package barcode is scanned into the courier system, our intake log automatically registers your return.

3. Instant 24h Release

Once checked for major burns or missing jewelry, the hold is released immediately. Normal wear, minor spills, and routine makeup dust are 100% covered under our complimentary care!

Fair Wear & Tear Criteria

100% Covered Under Deposit (Zero Extra Charge)
  • • Champagne, wine, or beverage splashes
  • • Makeup, foundation, or glitter transfer
  • • Loose seams, detached buttons, or popped eyelets
  • • Minor hem dirt from walking on outdoor red carpets
  • • Natural creasing or velvet pile pressure
Incurs Deposit Deduction (Severe Damage)
  • • Large cigarette burns or open flame singeing
  • • Irreparable structural scissors/blade cuts
  • • Missing high-value tiaras, masks, or armor pieces
  • • Unreturned garments after 14 days without notice
