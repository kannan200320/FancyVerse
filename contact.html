Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers regarding security deposits, fitting salon bookings, fit assurance, courier boxes, and theatrical production rentals.

How does the refundable security deposit work?

Every luxury rental includes a transparent, fully refundable security deposit authorized at checkout. Once your costume is returned to our Broadway salon or scanned into our courier return stream, our conservators inspect the piece within 48 hours. If the garment is free from permanent structural damage, unrecoverable tears, or missing ornate jewels, your deposit is automatically released back to your original payment method.

What happens if my costume does not fit upon delivery? (Fit Assurance)

We understand that gala and theatrical attire demands precision. Every courier rental arrives with backup corset laces, discreet seamstress clips, and body-adhesion tape. If an outfit does not fit comfortably, contact our emergency stylist line within 4 hours of delivery. We will dispatch an alternate size via expedited courier or issue an immediate 100% store credit.

How do private Broadway Salon fittings work? ($45 Credited to Rental)

Private 60-minute fitting sessions are conducted in our 4 private salon chambers at 480 Broadway Ave. For $45, our master corsetières and stylists measure your silhouette, provide temporary hemline adjustments, and serve artisanal espresso or champagne. Best of all, your entire $45 appointment fee is instantly credited toward your final costume rental!

How does courier delivery and prepaid return work?

Your ensemble arrives in a signature breathable garment carrier box with protective garment bags and a pre-printed, prepaid return label. When your rental window concludes, simply pack the garments back into the box, affix the included peel-and-stick label, and hand it to any UPS/FedEx courier or return it directly to our Broadway salon desk. Dry cleaning is handled entirely by us!

Can FancyVerse supply costumes for theatrical troupes and school productions?

Absolutely. We regularly outfit regional theater companies, high school drama departments, and corporate galas with bulk costume packages ranging from 5 to 50+ sets. We offer dedicated production managers, tiered volume discounts, flexible dress rehearsal extensions, and coordinated size-sorting crates.
