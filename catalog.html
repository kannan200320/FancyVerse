Fit Precision Guide

Selecting Your Perfect Size With Confidence

Theatrical and period garments fit differently than modern ready-to-wear fast fashion. Take 60 seconds with a soft tape measure to pinpoint your optimal fit before reserving.

±2 Inches of Built-In Corset Flexibility

Over 75% of our adult gowns and doublet coats incorporate internal bone stays with adjustable lacing panels, allowing easy custom tightening or loosening.

Comprehensive Size Guide Book In-Person Fitting
1

Chest / Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping tape comfortably horizontal. Wear the bra or undergarments you plan to use at your event.

2

Natural Waist

Locate your natural waistline (narrowest contour, usually 1 to 2 inches above your navel). Keep one finger between tape and body for breathing ease.

3

Hips & Seat

Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest circumference of your hips and glutes. Crucial for form-fitting flapper and mermaid gowns.

4

Length & Inseam

Measure from collarbone down to desired hem length, always taking your event footwear and heel height into calculation to avoid trailing hems.
