Everything You Need To Know

Fitting Studio FAQs

When you reserve an in-salon session, you pay a $45 appointment retainer. When you choose your costume, $45 is automatically deducted from your rental charge on your checkout receipt. If you don't book on the day, the credit remains valid in your account for 6 months.
To preserve the archival integrity of our period garments, we do not perform permanent cut alterations. Instead, our corsetières utilize specialized basting pins, temporary blind-stitching, padded inserts, and flexible spiral lacing to achieve an immaculate bespoke fit without cutting the fabric.
We recommend wearing seamless neutral undergarments and bringing the shoes or heel height you plan to wear at your event so we can align hemline lengths accurately.
We dispatch your selected costume in 3 consecutive size variations (e.g. S, M, L) in a reinforced travel case. You have 48 hours to try them on in the comfort of your home. Keep the perfect size, pack the remaining pieces back into the case, and leave it with your doorman or schedule a free courier pickup with our prepaid label.
