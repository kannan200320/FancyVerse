Atelier Measurement Guide

How To Measure Yourself Accurately

Use a flexible tape measure while wearing lightweight undergarments.

1

Bust / Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor under your arms.

2

Natural Waist

Measure around your narrowest point (usually 1-2 inches above your belly button). Do not suck in your breath!

3

Full Hips

Stand with heels together and measure around the widest part of your hips and buttocks.

4

Total Height & Inseam

Measure barefoot from top of head to floor for floor-length ballgowns, and from crotch to ankle for trousers.
